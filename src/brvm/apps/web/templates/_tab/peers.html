Ratio glossary — formulas + reading guide
P/E Price / earnings per share. How many years of current EPS the market is paying for a share. Lower = cheaper on earnings; negative EPS → suppressed (a negative multiple is a trap for a skim-reader).
ROE Net income / total equity. Return the company generates on shareholder capital. Sector-relative — banks/insurers structurally sit higher than industrials.
NET MARG Net income / revenue. How many CFA of profit per CFA of revenue. Reflects operating efficiency + tax + non-op items combined.
P/FCF Market cap / free cash flow. Cash-flow analog of P/E. Suppressed when FCF is negative (a negative multiple is meaningless). Complements P/E because accounting earnings and cash generation can diverge.
FCF YLD Free cash flow / market cap, in %. Cash-flow yield to the equity holder. Negative values are shown (informative: the company is burning cash). Compare against local risk-free rates + coupon yields on the Bonds tab.
EV/EBITDA* Enterprise value / EBITDA. Capital-structure-neutral valuation multiple. * BRVM issuers don't reliably publish net debt or D&A yet, so this uses EV ≈ market cap and EBITDA ≈ operating income. Treat as a proxy — under-reports EV for indebted issuers and under-reports EBITDA for capex-heavy ones.
MEDIAN / MEAN Central tendency across non-self peers. Both stay blank when fewer than 2 peers report a field (a single sample isn't a sector reference).
